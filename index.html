How to apply

Five steps, in this order.

  1. Check eligibility

    Match your 12th-grade subjects and marks with the programme table above.

  2. Fill in the online form

    Use the form below. It takes about ten minutes.

  3. Upload documents

    Mark sheets for classes 10 and 12, transfer certificate, ID proof and a passport-size photo.

  4. Pay the application fee

    ₹500 by UPI or card. The fee is not refundable.

  5. Attend counselling

    Shortlisted students are called for document verification and seat allotment.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an entrance exam?

No. Admission is based on 12th-grade marks. Engineering applicants may submit a JEE or state CET score instead.

Can I change my programme after applying?

Yes, until the merit list is published on 15 May. Email admissions@greenfield.example with your application number.

Are hostel rooms guaranteed?

First-year students get priority. Rooms are allotted after fee payment.

Do you offer education loan support?

Yes. The accounts office provides the documents that major banks ask for.
